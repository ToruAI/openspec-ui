# Proposal

## Why

Plant returns are our largest refund reason ("it died"), and most could be avoided with basic care guidance that today only exists in the packaging insert.

## What Changes

- Show light, water and pet-safety badges on every plant product page
- Add a collapsible care guide with watering schedule
- Link the guide from the shipping email

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `product-catalog`: Product attributes and detail page content

### Modified Capabilities


## Impact

- `products` gains structured care attributes
- Content team fills in care data for ~140 plants
